# Smart-Palletization-of-Kuka-Robot-by-integrating-vision-system

For my B.E. final year project, our team of four automated palletizing of 2.0 kg porous cardboard boxes using a KUKA KR 6 R900 robot.

I helped design a custom vacuum gripper from first principles, with a 118.86 N holding force, four 40 mm suction cups at −0.6 bar and a Techno ZK-15 ejector. Calculations matched datasheet curves within 1%. The cycle is programmed in KUKA KRL with proximity and vision sensing.

Results: no slip at 2.0 kg, vacuum in 0.25 s, reliable detection over 10+ cycles.

Skills: KUKA KRL · Industrial Robotics · Pneumatics · Sensor Integration
